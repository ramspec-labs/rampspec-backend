# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in RampSpec a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing opinions, constructive feedback, accountability, and focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling or insulting comments, harassment, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the repository administrators through the RampSpec Labs GitHub organization. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers must respect the privacy and security of reporters.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and free from conflicts of interest.
